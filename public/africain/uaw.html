The Pioneers

In 1995, a group of Club Africain supporters in Tunis did something that had never been done before in their country, or anywhere in the Arab world: they organized themselves into the first ultra group in North Africa and the Arab world — African Winners.

Inspired by the Italian ultra culture they had seen on satellite television, they took the concept — the organized curve, the tifo, the capo, the chants — and transplanted it into Tunisian football. What grew from that seed transformed the atmosphere of Tunisian stadiums forever.

African Winners did not just create a fan group. They created a movement. Every ultra group that followed in Tunisia — and every ultra group across the Arab world — stands on the foundation they laid in 1995.

"TIFO 3D By African Winners 1995 — the first ultras group in Tunisia, which belongs to Club Africain." — Les Rebelles du Foot (Facebook page dedicated to North African ultra culture)

Why "Winners"?

Club Africain
❤
Wydad AC

The name carries weight. Club Africain had, just four years before the group's founding, achieved the greatest result in Tunisian football history: winning the 1991 CAF African Cup of Champions Clubs — the premier continental club competition, predecessor to today's CAF Champions League. They were the winners of Africa.

The name "African Winners" is both a statement of fact and a declaration of identity. We support the African winners. We are the winners. The name links the group permanently to the club's greatest continental triumph, carrying the memory of 1991 into every match, every tifo, every chant.

It is also — and this matters — a name that travels. When the Moroccan ultra group of Wydad Casablanca chose the name Winners 2005 a decade later, they knew what they were referencing: a North African ultra tradition, a red football club, a shared sense of ambition. Winners know each other.

The Virage Nord — Home of the Winners

At the Stade Olympique de Radès, the north curve — the Virage Nord — belongs to Club Africain's ultra groups. This is the territory of African Winners and their fellow organized groups: Leaders Clubistes, North Vandals, Dodgers Clubistes, and others who share the same red and white identity.

When the teams emerge from the tunnel, the Virage Nord erupts. The capo, back to the pitch, facing his own fans, arms raised — directing the opening chant that rolls across the entire stand and eventually shakes the whole stadium. The tifo unfurls. Flares ignite. Sixty thousand people feel it simultaneously.

On derby night — Club Africain vs. Espérance — the Virage Nord reaches its peak. The noise, the smoke, the sheer collective will directed at ninety minutes of football. This is what African Winners built. This is what they maintain, year after year, result after result.

"The north curve never sits down. Not at 3-0 up. Not at 0-3 down. Never."

The Identity of African Winners

🏁

Tifo

African Winners introduced the concept of organized tifo to Tunisian football. Giant banners, card sections, coordinated color — they built the visual vocabulary of Tunisian ultra culture.

🔴

Red & White

The colors of Club Africain. Every banner, scarf, and flare signal the same identity: red passion, white pride. The colors that represent Tunis on the African continent.

🌌

Pioneer Status

First in Tunisia. First in the Arab world. African Winners carry this history not as a trophy, but as a responsibility — the responsibility to maintain the highest standards of support.

🤝

Brotherhood

Inside the Virage Nord, group differences fade. Outside the stadium, the bond continues — away trips, tifos, fundraising, and the solidarity that defines ultra brotherhood across borders.

What It Means to Be a Winner

  • ★
    You chose Club Africain. Not because they were winning the league that year. Not because it was fashionable. Because something in the red and white called to you, and you answered.
  • ★
    You show up. Home games, away games. Cold nights in Radès. Hostile grounds in other cities. When the rest of the world stays home, you go.
  • ★
    You contribute. Painting banners until 3am before a derby. Helping carry tifo materials. Funding flares out of your own pocket. The Virage Nord does not build itself.
  • ★
    You remember 1991. The CAF Champions League title is not ancient history — it is the living heart of this group's identity. "Winners" is not just a name. It is a claim about what Club Africain is, and what it will be again.
  • ★
    You carry the tradition. African Winners was the first. You are part of a lineage that started in 1995 and extends forward to every future generation that stands in the Virage Nord and sings for the red and white.
